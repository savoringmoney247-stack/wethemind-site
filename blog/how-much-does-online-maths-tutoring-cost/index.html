How Much Does Online Maths Tutoring Cost? What to Look For

4 min read

Prices for online tutoring vary a lot, and the cheapest hour is not always the best value. Here is how to compare fairly.

What changes the price

  • One-to-one or group. A group splits the teacher's time, so it costs less per student.
  • Level and exam. Advanced courses such as AP Calculus, IB HL or A-level Further Maths ask for a more specialised teacher.
  • Package or single class. Buying a bundle usually lowers the price of each class.
  • What is included. Marked homework, recordings, progress notes and a trial class all add real value.

Our prices, in plain numbers

Your first 30-minute class is free. After that, small-group classes start from $5.9 per student per class, one-to-one classes start from $9.9 per class in a package, and a single one-to-one class is $19. Homework help, where we solve up to 5 problems you are stuck on, is one session at $19. All prices are on our prices page, shown in your own currency.

A simple budget rule

One class a week plus marked practice is a steady rhythm. Add extra classes only in the weeks before an exam.

A checklist before you pay

  • Is there a free trial class on a real question?
  • Does the tutor teach your exact board or exam?
  • Is homework marked and explained?
  • Can you reschedule without a fee? (We allow it free up to 4 hours before.)
  • What is the refund rule, in writing? (Ours is the exact amount except the charges.)
  • Will they contact you by sales calls, or only when you ask?
