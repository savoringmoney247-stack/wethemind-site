Fill the grid so every row, column and 2 by 2 box has the numbers 1 to 4 exactly once. A gentle way to learn Sudoku.

How to play

  1. Pick a squareTap any empty square. Fixed numbers are shaded.
  2. Pick a numberTap 1, 2, 3 or 4 below the grid.
  3. No repeatsEach row, column and 2 by 2 box needs 1 to 4 once.
  4. Need help?The Hint button fills one square for you.

Did you know? Sudoku needs no maths at all, only logic. The numbers could be colours or letters and the puzzle would work the same.

Questions people ask

Is there always one answer?

Yes. Every puzzle here has exactly one solution, so you never have to guess.

Why start with 4 by 4?

It teaches the rules in a few minutes. After that, the 9 by 9 puzzles in newspapers feel much friendlier.

Is it free?

Yes. No sign-up and no download.
