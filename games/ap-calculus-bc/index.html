Questions people ask

What is extra in BC compared with AB?

Integration by parts, partial fractions, improper integrals, Euler’s method, logistic growth, parametric, polar and vector calculus, series convergence tests and Taylor series.

Can I use it for AB too?

Yes. The first camps review AB ideas, then BC topics take over.

Are the questions the same each time?

No. Numbers and pictures are generated fresh every time you play a camp.
