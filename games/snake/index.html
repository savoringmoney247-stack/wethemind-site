Play Snake

Steer the snake to eat apples. Each apple makes you longer and a little faster. Do not hit the wall or your own tail!

Press Start, then use the arrow keys, WASD or swipe.

How to play

  1. Press StartThe snake begins moving right.
  2. SteerUse arrow keys, WASD, swipe, or the on-screen buttons.
  3. Eat applesEach apple adds one point and one body part.
  4. Stay safeHitting a wall or your own tail ends the game.

Did you know? Snake first became famous on mobile phones in the late 1990s. It is still a great way to practise planning ahead.

Questions people ask

How do I play on a phone?

Swipe on the board or tap the arrow buttons below it.

Why does it speed up?

Every few apples the snake gets a little faster to keep it exciting.

Is it free?

Yes. No sign-up and no download.
