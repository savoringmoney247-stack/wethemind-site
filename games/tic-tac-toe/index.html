How to play

  1. Pick a modeChoose easy, hard, or two players.
  2. Take turnsYou are X. Tap a square to place your mark.
  3. Three in a rowMake a line of three: across, down, or on a diagonal.
  4. Block!Stop your opponent when they have two in a row.

Did you know? On hard mode the computer never makes a mistake. The best you can do is draw. Can you find a way to never lose?

Questions people ask

Can you always win at Tic Tac Toe?

No. If both players play perfectly, the game is always a draw. Winning needs the other player to make a mistake.

What is the best first move?

Taking the centre or a corner gives you the most chances to make a line.

Is it free to play?

Yes. It is free with no sign-up.
