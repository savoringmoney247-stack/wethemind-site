Play Four in a Row

Take turns dropping discs. Connect four of your colour in a row, column or diagonal to win. Play the computer or a friend.

Tap a column to drop your disc.

How to play

  1. Pick a modeEasy, Hard, or Two players on the same screen.
  2. Drop a discTap any column. The disc falls to the lowest free spot.
  3. Make fourLine up four in a row, column or diagonal.
  4. Block!Stop your opponent when they have three in a row.

Did you know? With perfect play, the player who goes first can always win Four in a Row. Starting in the middle column is the strongest first move.

Questions people ask

What is a good first move?

Drop your first disc in the middle column. It is part of the most winning lines.

Can I play with a friend?

Yes. Choose Two players and take turns on the same screen.

Is it free?

Yes. No sign-up and no download.
