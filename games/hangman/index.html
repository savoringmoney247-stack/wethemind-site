How to play

  1. Pick a level and a packEasy has short words and 8 lives. Pick animals, science, maths or food.
  2. Guess a letterTap a letter. If it is in the word, it appears. If not, a part of the drawing is added.
  3. Watch the drawingYou can make 6 wrong guesses. After that, the round is over.
  4. Spell it outFill in every letter before the drawing is finished to win.

Did you know? Good Hangman players start with the most common letters: E, A, R, S, T. Vowels are a smart first guess too.

Questions people ask

What is the best first letter in Hangman?

E is the most common letter in English words. A, R, S and T are also good first guesses.

Does Hangman help with spelling?

Yes. You think about which letters can go together, which builds spelling and vocabulary.
