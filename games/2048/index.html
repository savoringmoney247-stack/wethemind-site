Play 2048 Free

Slide the number tiles. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one bigger tile. Can you make 2048?

How to play

  1. SlideUse the arrow keys, swipe, or tap the arrow buttons. All tiles slide the same way.
  2. MergeTwo matching tiles join and add up: 2 and 2 make 4. 4 and 4 make 8.
  3. Plan aheadKeep your biggest tile in a corner. Think two moves ahead.
  4. Reach 2048Make a 2048 tile to win. You can keep playing after that!

Did you know? Every tile is a power of 2: 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 and so on. The numbers double each time. That is the same idea as exponents in maths: 211 = 2048.

Questions people ask

How do I win at 2048?

Keep your biggest tile in one corner, keep moving in two or three directions, and avoid sliding the way that pulls it out of the corner.

Is this 2048 free?

Yes, it is free, with no download and no sign-up. It saves your best score on your own device.

Can I play 2048 on my phone?

Yes. Swipe on the board, or tap the arrow buttons below it.
