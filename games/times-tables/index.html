How to play

  1. Pick a levelPractice has no timer. Try it first, then race the clock.
  2. Press StartThe 60-second clock starts. A multiplication question appears.
  3. Type the answerType it and press Enter. Right answers score a point and build a streak.
  4. Check your missesAt the end you see the questions you missed, so you can learn them.

Did you know? Here is a trick for the 9 times table: the digits of every answer add up to 9. 9 × 4 = 36, and 3 + 6 = 9. Try it!

Questions people ask

How can I learn my times tables fast?

Practise a little every day, start with the easy tables (2, 5, 10), then move on. Short games like this one work better than one long session.

Which times tables should I start with?

Start with 2, 5 and 10, then 3 and 4, then the harder ones like 7, 8 and 9.

Is this times tables game free?

Yes. It is free with no sign-up. Your best score is saved on your own device.
