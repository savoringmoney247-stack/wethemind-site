01How can I raise my SAT Math score by 100 points?SAT

Fix the mistakes you repeat. Keep an error log and sort each miss as careless, content gap or time. Most points come back from a handful of topics: linear systems, quadratics, percent and data.

02How does the Digital SAT adapt to me?SAT

Math has two 35-minute modules of 22 questions each. How you do in Module 1 decides how hard Module 2 is, so Module 1 matters most. Slow down there.

Free SAT practice sets →
03How do I use Desmos on the SAT?SAT

The graphing calculator is built in. Type both sides of an equation and read where the lines cross, or plug each answer choice in to test it.

Try practice sets →
04How long should I spend on each SAT Math question?SAT

About 95 seconds on average (70 minutes for 44 questions). If one stalls past about 90 seconds, flag it and come back.

05AP Calculus AB or BC: which one should I take?AP Calculus

AB covers limits, derivatives and integrals. BC adds series, parametric and polar work, so it is a faster and bigger course. If you plan to study engineering or science, BC is a strong pick.

06What is a derivative, in plain words?AP Calculus

How fast something is changing right now. A car's speedometer is a derivative of its distance.

07Why do I get lost in related rates and optimisation?AP Calculus

They are word problems first and calculus second. Draw it, name every quantity, write one equation, then differentiate. We practise that order until it is automatic.

09GCSE, IGCSE, A-level, IB: what is the difference?GCSE · A-level · IB · IGCSE

GCSE and A-level are the UK system. IGCSE is the international cousin of GCSE. IB is a two-year diploma with maths at Analysis and Approaches or Applications and Interpretation level, standard or higher.

10How do I move from GCSE to A-level Maths without a shock?GCSE · A-level · IB · IGCSE

A-level asks for algebra fluency and proof-style thinking. Build algebra and graph skills over the summer before you start.

11IB Maths: AA or AI, SL or HL?GCSE · A-level · IB · IGCSE

AA is for algebra, calculus and proof, often for maths, science or engineering degrees. AI is for data, modelling and applied work. HL goes deeper than SL.

IB Maths →
12Which IGCSE tier should I aim for?GCSE · A-level · IB · IGCSE

Extended gives access to the top grades. Core is capped lower. If you are unsure, start preparing for Extended and decide with your teacher.

13Maths Standard or Maths Basic for Class 10?CBSE Class 9–10

Standard suits students heading to Maths or Science in Class 11. Basic is a gentler paper for those who will not study maths further. Choose with your teacher before the school deadline.

14What are competency-based and case-based questions?CBSE Class 9–10

Questions that test whether you can use an idea in a real situation, not only repeat a formula. The Section E case studies are worth 12 marks in the Class 10 paper.

15Where do most of the marks come from?CBSE Class 9–10

In the Class 10 Standard paper, Algebra is worth the most (20 of 80), then Geometry (15) and Trigonometry (12). Do these three well first.

16How do I start preparing for the AMC 8, 10 or 12?AMC · ACT · TIFR

Begin with past papers from the level just below your own. The AMC 10 and 12 give you 75 minutes for 25 questions, so learn to skip and return.

17What does the ACT Math section feel like?AMC · ACT · TIFR

Fast and wide. It touches algebra, geometry and trig with little time to think, so speed on basics wins points.

18What is TIFR and who should take it?AMC · ACT · TIFR

It is an entrance route to advanced mathematics study in India. It rewards deep understanding of undergraduate maths, not tricks.
