Why We Start Early

Grade 9 Is Where the Advantage Gets Built

Every board exam, entrance test, and college application after this point rests on what gets locked in right now. Start strong here, and every year that follows gets easier — not harder.

Grade 9–10: The Foundation Locks In

Algebra, functions, and core science ideas taught here are exactly what every later exam is built on. Get them right the first time, not the third.

Grade 11–12: The Advantage Compounds

Students with a solid Grade 9–10 base sail through AP, A-Levels, and board finals — instead of relearning fundamentals while also trying to keep up with new material.

SAT / ACT: Ready Years Before Test Day

Starting early means test prep is a natural extension of what a student already knows — not a stressful cram session squeezed into junior year.

College & Beyond: A Head Start That Lasts

The habits and subject fluency built from Grade 9 carry straight into university coursework — first-year calculus and physics feel like review, not a wall.

Questions

Before you book

Is the first session really free?+

Yes — 30 minutes, no card required. We only talk about paid plans after the session, and only if you want to continue.

What if my child's board isn't listed?+

Select "Other" in the form and tell us the board and country — we're actively adding curricula and will let you know if we can match a mentor.

What happens after the demo?+

Your mentor shares a short note on what was covered and what to focus on next. If you'd like to continue, we'll walk you through plans and pricing then — never before.

Are sessions recorded?+

Recording is optional and off by default. If you'd like a copy for revision, let your mentor know before the session starts.

Can I reschedule or cancel?+

Yes, free of charge, any time before the session — just reply to your confirmation email.
