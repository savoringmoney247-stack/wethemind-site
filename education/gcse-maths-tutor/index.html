Exam format at a glance

PapersThree papers: Paper 1 non-calculator, Papers 2 and 3 calculator
Marks and time80 marks and 1 hour 30 minutes per paper
TiersFoundation covers grades 1 to 5; Higher covers grades 4 to 9
BoardsEdexcel, AQA, OCR — we check your board's spec and past papers

What we cover

  • Number, ratio and proportion
  • Algebra: expanding, factorising, equations and graphs
  • Geometry and measures, including Pythagoras and trigonometry
  • Probability and statistics
  • Frequency polygons and cumulative frequency
  • Formulae, functions and sequences
  • Non-calculator technique for Paper 1
  • Higher: circle theorems, vectors, algebraic proof

A typical 4-week plan

  1. Week 1: diagnostic past-paper question set to find weak topics
  2. Week 2: topic teaching with exam-style questions
  3. Week 3: mixed practice and non-calculator technique
  4. Week 4: full timed paper and a marks-by-topic review

How classes work

Every class is live and interactive on a shared digital whiteboard. Choose one-to-one mentoring, or a small group of 2–10 students on the same board and subject. Your first 30-minute class is free, and prices are on the pricing page.

Frequently asked questions

Which GCSE boards do you teach?

Edexcel, AQA and OCR maths, at Foundation and Higher tier.

How many marks do I need for a grade 4?

Grade boundaries change each year and by board and tier. Check your exam board's published boundaries; we help you target the topics that move your mark most.

Can you help with the non-calculator paper?

Yes. Paper 1 technique (fractions, surds, mental methods, estimating) is a regular focus.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, the first 30-minute class is free.
