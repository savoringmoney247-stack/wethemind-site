Question typesMCQs, very short, short and long answers, case-based questions

A typical 4-week plan

  1. Week 1: concept gaps — rebuild the chapters your child finds hardest using NCERT examples
  2. Week 2: NCERT exercises plus exemplar problems, with mistakes logged
  3. Week 3: chapter tests and case-based questions
  4. Week 4: timed full sample paper, then a step-marking review

How classes work

Every class is live and interactive on a shared digital whiteboard. Choose one-to-one mentoring, or a small group of 2–10 students on the same board and subject. Your first 30-minute class is free, and prices are on the pricing page.
