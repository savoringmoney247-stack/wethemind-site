How to play Lexigyre

Unscramble the word. Every right answer turns the puzzle cube back one move, until the whole cube is solved.

  1. 1. Look at the lettersThey are mixed up. Tap them in an order that spells a real word.
  2. 2. Get it rightGreen means a letter is in the right place. Orange means try again. A wrong guess costs nothing.
  3. 3. Watch the cube turnEach correct word undoes one scramble move. Finish the word list to see the cube solve itself.
  4. 4. Level upWords get longer and the cube gets trickier. Come back each day for the Daily puzzle.

Questions kids and parents ask

Is it free?

Yes. It is free to play in your browser, with no sign-up.

Does it save my place?

Yes. Your level and score are saved on your own device, so you can close it and come back.

Does it teach anything?

It builds vocabulary and spelling, and it trains you to think a few steps ahead, like a cube solver. Some words come from maths and science.

Why does it make sound?

Sound starts after your first tap. Tap the speaker icon to turn it off.
