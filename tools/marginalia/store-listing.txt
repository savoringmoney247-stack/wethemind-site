FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
------------------------------------------------------
Marginalia is a simple, fast PDF markup tool for anyone who needs to annotate, edit, or mark up a document without touching the original file.

No account. No sign-up. No uploads to a server — everything happens right on your device.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Highlight text and passages
• Add sticky-note style annotations and comments
• Cover existing text and retype over it
• Insert images anywhere on a page
• Add extra blank writing pages to any PDF
• Rotate, reorder, and navigate pages easily
• Search across the whole document
• Zoom in/out and use a clean, distraction-free reading view
• Full undo/redo support, so you can experiment freely
• Export your marked-up PDF, ready to share or print

WHO IT'S FOR
Teachers marking up worksheets, students annotating study material, professionals reviewing contracts and reports, or anyone who just needs to write on a PDF without installing a full desktop suite.

PRIVACY-FIRST BY DESIGN
Marginalia works entirely on the files you open — locally, on your device. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or shared. Your documents stay yours.

Marginalia is built and maintained by We The Mind (wethemind.com).
