Privacy Policy — Manaluz (PDF Markup)

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Manaluz is a PDF markup tool published by We The Mind. This policy explains how the app and the website version handle your information.

Your files stay yours

Manaluz works entirely on files you open within the app, on your own device. PDFs, images, and any edits you make are processed locally in your browser or device and are not uploaded to, or stored on, any server operated by We The Mind or Manaluz.

Accounts and sign-in

You can use Manaluz's free features without signing up. Paid plans (Ember, Beacon, Aurora and Lifetime) may ask you to sign in with your Google account so your plan can follow you between devices. When you sign in, Google shares your name, email address and account ID with us. We use them only to recognise your plan, and we store them with Google Firebase (Google Cloud). We do not use them for marketing and we do not sell them. To have your sign-in data deleted, email us at the address below.

Ads

The free version of Manaluz shows ads served by Google (Google AdMob in the Android app, and Google AdSense on the website where ads are shown). Google and its partners may use your device's advertising ID or cookies to show and measure ads, and to show ads that are relevant to you where your settings allow it. We do not receive your name or contact details through ads. You can reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads) or at adssettings.google.com. Learn how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for their choice through Google's consent message. Paid plans remove or reduce ads as described in the app.

Purchases and payments

In the Android app, purchases are handled by Google Play (Google Play Billing), under Google's own terms and privacy policy. On computers, where paid plans are offered, payments are handled by Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking). We never see or store your card or bank details. We only receive confirmation that a payment was made and which plan it covers.

Third-party services

Manaluz uses these third-party services: Google Play (downloads, reviews, purchases), Google AdMob and AdSense (ads), Google Sign-In and Firebase (sign-in and plan records), and Razorpay (payments on computers). Each handles information under its own privacy policy, including Google's Privacy Policy. We do not sell your personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Manaluz's features evolve. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@wethemind.com.
