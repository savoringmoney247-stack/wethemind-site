Pricing Questions

Before you choose a plan

Can I book just one session to solve specific problems?+

Yes — the Single Problem-Solving Session is a flat $20, one-time, no package required. Send your list of up to 5 problems when you book, and your mentor comes prepared to work through them with you.

Can my child study with friends in a group?+

Yes — group sessions fit 2 to 10 students on the same board and subject, with one mentor and one live session. The cost per student drops as the group grows. Mention your group size on the booking form and we'll match a mentor who can teach all of you together.

Are there any hidden charges?+

None — what you see on the pricing table is what you pay. No booking fee, no "platform charge," no surprise line item hiding in the fine print.
